# Example Company Handbook
Fictional demonstration document. This is not an actual employer policy.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week after their manager approves the arrangement. Requests must be submitted at least three working days before the first remote day. Customer support coverage must be maintained during business hours.

## Equipment requests
Employees submit equipment requests to the IT service desk. Requests must include the device type, business justification, and manager approval. Standard requests are reviewed within five working days.

## Password security
Employees must use multi-factor authentication for company accounts. Passwords must never be shared with colleagues. Suspected account compromise must be reported immediately to the IT service desk.
